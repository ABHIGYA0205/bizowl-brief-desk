# PaySetu: approved brief

Approved in brief-desk by a BizOwl team member.

**Snapshot:** a pre-seed fintech app that lets kirana stores in Tier-3 towns give and track small customer credit, with UPI reminders. Raising Rs 40 lakh.

**Problem:** kirana stores in Tier-3 towns can't offer credit to regular customers, so they lose sales.

**Solution:** a simple app for kiranas to give and track small customer credit, with UPI reminders.

**Traction (3 months):** 340 kiranas using the app. Rs 1.1 crore of credit tracked through the app, which is credit the kiranas recorded, not PaySetu's revenue or GMV.

**Team:** the CEO was previously in merchant operations at Paytm. The CTO is a former Razorpay engineer.

**The ask:** Rs 40 lakh. 50% for product (Rs 20 lakh), 30% for sales (Rs 12 lakh), 20% for compliance (Rs 8 lakh). The rupee amounts are worked out from the percentages.

**To confirm:** revenue and the business model, active versus registered kiranas, repayment rates, market size and towns served, competitors, and why now.

## Key risks (analyst's reading, not founder claims)

- **Revenue not reported:** the application doesn't report revenue. It may or may not have any.
- **Credit risk:** we don't know yet whether PaySetu only tracks credit or also funds or underwrites it.
- **Regulation:** 20% of the raise goes to compliance, and which rules apply is not confirmed.
- **Short track record:** 3 months of data. Retention and how often kiranas use it are not confirmed.
