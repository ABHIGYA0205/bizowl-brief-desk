# SkillSetu: approved brief

Approved in brief-desk by a BizOwl team member.

**Snapshot:** a seed-stage edtech company running 8-week hybrid job-skills courses for Tier-3 graduates, with local placement tie-ups. Raising Rs 1.5 crore.

**Problem:** graduates in Tier-3 towns lack job-ready digital skills, and local employers can't find trained staff.

**Solution:** 8-week hybrid courses in digital marketing and accounting, with placement tie-ups with local businesses.

**Traction (12 months):** 620 students trained, a reported 71% placement rate (how it's measured is not confirmed), and Rs 38 lakh revenue.

**Team:** the CEO was previously at upGrad. The CTO is a former Byju's product engineer.

**The ask:** Rs 1.5 crore. 40% for new centres (Rs 60 lakh), 35% for content (Rs 52.5 lakh), 25% for the placement team (Rs 37.5 lakh). The rupee amounts are worked out from the percentages.

**To confirm:** how many centres there are now and where, the business model and price per course, market size and competitors, and why now.

## Key risks (analyst's reading, not founder claims)

- **Placement claim:** how the 71% is defined, which students it covers, and whether it's verified are not confirmed.
- **Centre expansion:** 40% of the raise funds new centres. The current footprint and the economics of each centre are not confirmed.
- **Employer demand:** placements depend on local tie-ups. How many employers there are and how many people they can hire are not confirmed.
- **Unit economics:** pricing, cost per student and margins are not in the application.
