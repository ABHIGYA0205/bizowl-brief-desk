# SkillSetu: approved intro emails

Banyan Ventures and Kaveri Impact Fund were approved. Indus Tech Fund was drafted as a weak fit and not approved, so it is not here.

## Banyan Ventures

**Why:** it backs edtech at seed stage, Rs 1-5 crore per deal, so all three fit. It wants 6+ months of revenue, and SkillSetu reports 12.

**Subject:** SkillSetu, seed-stage edtech for Tier-3 job skills, raising Rs 1.5 crore

Hi Banyan Ventures team,

I'd like to introduce SkillSetu, a seed-stage edtech startup. Graduates in Tier-3 towns lack job-ready digital skills, and local employers can't find trained staff. SkillSetu runs 8-week hybrid courses in digital marketing and accounting, with placement tie-ups with local businesses. Over 12 months it has trained 620 students, reports a 71% placement rate and has earned Rs 38 lakh revenue. The CEO was previously at upGrad, and the CTO is a former Byju's product engineer. SkillSetu is raising Rs 1.5 crore for new centres, content and its placement team.

Would you be open to a short call? I can share the full brief.

Best, BizOwl team
