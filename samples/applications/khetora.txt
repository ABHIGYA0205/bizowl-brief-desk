Problem and market
The problem
Small farmers in Madhya Pradesh sell soybean and wheat at the mandi without knowing the grade of their crop, so traders underpay them by 10-15%. Grading labs are far away and slow.
Target market
Farmer producer organisations in central India, and the traders and processors who buy from them.
Market size
Founder's estimate: India has over 10,000 registered FPOs, and quality testing is a growing need as more FPOs sell directly to processors.
